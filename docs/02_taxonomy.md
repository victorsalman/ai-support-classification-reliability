# Taxonomy

The taxonomy used in this case study is a simulated retailer specific business taxonomy. These boundaries are not universal definitions.

## Order Status

Questions or concerns about tracking, shipping progress, delivery status, delivery timing, or an order that has not arrived.

## Return or Refund

Requests or questions about returning an item, receiving a refund, or the return process.

## Damaged or Incorrect Item

Use when the customer explicitly or clearly indicates damage, defect, the wrong item, wrong variant, wrong size, wrong quantity, missing expected components, or another stated mismatch.

Dissatisfaction, anger, or reference to receiving something is not by itself evidence of this category.

## Cancellation

Requests or questions about cancelling an order.

## Payment or Billing

Payment failures, duplicate charges, billing questions, invoices, unexpected charges, or payment method issues.

## Account Access

Problems signing in, passwords, account access, or authentication.

## Product Question

Questions about the features, use, compatibility, specifications, or availability of merchandise.

## Other

A clear support request that does not fit another approved category.

For this simulated retailer, gift card availability, loyalty programs, store credit availability, and similar non merchandise services belong under Other unless another category clearly applies.

Examples:

`Do you sell gift cards?` maps to Other.

`My gift card keeps getting declined.` maps to Payment or Billing.

`Can I get my refund issued to a gift card?` maps to Return or Refund.

## Unclassifiable Messages

If no support issue can be identified reliably, both category arrays remain empty. A vague or angry message should not be forced into Other simply because it is difficult to classify.
